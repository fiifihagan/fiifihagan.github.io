# Charles Fiifi Hagan | GRC Portfolio

Portfolio of Charles Fiifi Hagan, an IT governance, risk and compliance professional and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Auditor based in Accra, Ghana.

**Live site:** https://fiifihagan.github.io/

## About

I help organisations turn security frameworks into controls that stand up to an audit. My work covers gap and readiness assessments against ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2, NIST CSF and PCI DSS, security policies and procedures, third-party risk, and risk registers kept moving to closure. I apply the Ghana Data Protection Act (Act 843) and GDPR in healthcare, legal and non-profit settings.

I am the Founder and CEO of Chayil SecureX, a cybersecurity GRC consultancy.

## What the site contains

- **Profile:** background, services, and experience from 2017 to today
- **Work samples:** ISO 27001 ISMS model, GRC maturity self-assessments, and policy and governance documents (details below)
- **Certifications:** fifteen certifications across audit and ISMS, governance, privacy and AI, and security operations
- **Speaking and media:** MOBEX Africa 2026, GITEX Nigeria 2025, Fintech for Inclusion Africa Summit, and national media appearances

## Run locally

No tools or dependencies are needed. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit http://localhost:8000.

## Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder. Pushing a change to `main` publishes it within a minute or two.
